How to Play
Everything you do is music.

Move — drag anywhere. A joystick appears under your thumb.

Your voices attack on the beat. Pulse fires every beat, Thump bursts on the downbeat, Snare Arc cracks on 2 & 4, Crash rings out every other bar. Watch the four beat lights at the top and you'll feel it.

Your build is your band. Each voice adds an instrument to the soundtrack. Every Hushling you silence plays a note — chain them into a harmony and the melody climbs, the drums thicken and the sky brightens, from piano to tutti. Getting hit breaks the chord.

Dash on the beat. Tap DASH (or tap with a second finger). A ring shrinks around your light — dash as it closes for a PERFECT: a shockwave, a breath of invulnerability and extra Crescendo.

Crescendo — when the ✺ ring glows, tap it to silence everything on screen and play your Singer's Solo — every Singer's is different.

Call & Response — sometimes the song plays a phrase on golden beats. Dash on exactly those beats in the next bar to answer it and earn a music box.

Grow. Collect light to level up. Pick new voices and harmonies. Two partner voices at their peak become a Duet. Elites and every Hush carry music boxes — walk into one to choose a Motif, a rule-bending gift for the rest of the song.

Read the rhythm of the Hush. Lurchers lunge on the downbeat. Wisps glow before they spit. Veils are shielded except on 2 & 4. Conductors hasten everything near them — silence them first. A Hush arrives every three minutes, and the song modulates up a key each time you break one.

Restore the song. At 9:00 The Great Silence arrives. It puts out the lights. Silence it and the song is restored — then keep singing in Endless, or take a bow.

Dynamics pay. Every new Dynamic you restore a song at pays a one-time bounty, lights a pip on the song card and frames your poster in bronze, silver, gold, prism or silenzio black. Restoring a song at fortissimo earns its Signature trail. Restoring it at silenzio crowns you its Virtuoso: +15% Echoes on that song, forever.

The Hush listens. If your song silences Hushlings the moment they appear, the Hush adapts with more and tougher Hushlings, and pays more Echoes for it. Each Hush now fights in three movements: at two-thirds and one-third health it holds its note, shrugging off damage while shaped marks fall, then falters. Its health grows with your power, and the last third of every song is a finale of shapes.

Signatures. Every song has its own Signature Hushling, a dangerous foe that appears only there: Dawn's Gilded Herald, Dusk's Weeping Veil, Aurora's Lightweavers, Abyss's Maw, Dream's Somnambulist, Tide's Leviathan, Ember's Bellows and Hallow's Hollow Lantern. Each one warns you before it strikes. Requiem brings them all. Harder songs hit harder, and mid-song the marks take shapes: lines, crosses, and the Fermata ring, where the only safe place is the still eye in the middle.

Rest Marks. The Hush aims at where you stand: a red ring fills for one bar and lands on the downbeat. Keep moving — or dash through it right on the beat for a Riposte. Late in a song, and in Endless, they come faster and in pairs. Cantors snipe from far away along a red line. In Endless, a new Mark of Silence curses the song every two minutes.

Living moments. Mid-song the music may surge into an Accelerando (faster, more experience), a Note Rain may fall, or a named Champion with three strange powers may come for you. Elites can carry powers too — read their tags.

Familiars follow you into every song — a finch that shoots on the beat, a moth that gathers light, a shell that swallows dark notes. Choose one in Resonance. Dissonances sometimes hide in music boxes: powerful gifts with a price.

Living songs. In Tide, currents sweep the whole field every few bars. In Ember, fires break out around you — they burn the Hush far worse than you, so lead them in.

More ways to sing. Modes holds the Recital (every Hush back to back), the Weekly Concert (three twists, new each week) and the Studio, where you set the rules. The Concert Hall remembers every song and your records. Make a Poster of any song to share it.

Come back louder. Each restored song unlocks a harder Dynamic for it, from p up to ∅ silenzio, with richer Echoes. Spend Echoes on Resonance, Singers and trails. Three Daily Verses and a Daily Hum wait every morning.

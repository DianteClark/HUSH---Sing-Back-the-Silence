How to Play
Everything you do is music.

Move — drag anywhere on the screen (or WASD / arrow keys). That's the only control you need.

Your voices attack on the beat. Pulse fires on every beat, Thump bursts on the downbeat, Snare Arc cracks on 2 & 4. Watch the four beat lights at the top and you'll feel it.

Your build is your band. Each new voice adds an instrument to the soundtrack. Every Hushling you silence plays a note — chain them into a harmony combo and the melody climbs higher. Getting hit breaks the chord.

Collect light dropped by fallen Hushlings to level up and choose new voices or harmonies. Tempo literally speeds up the song — and every weapon with it.

Dash on the beat. Tap DASH (or Shift, or tap with a second finger) to burst forward. Land it right on the beat — the shrinking ring around you shows when — for a PERFECT: a shockwave, a moment of invulnerability and extra Crescendo. Chain perfects for bragging rights.

Duets. When two particular voices both reach their peak, they can merge into a Duet — a new, stronger form. Your Songbook hints at which pairs belong together.

Crescendo — fill the ring and tap it (or Space) to unleash a wave that silences everything on screen.

Read the rhythm of the Hush. Lurchers gather on beat 4 and lunge on the downbeat. Wisps glow before they spit. The Hush itself arrives every three minutes — defeat it and the song modulates up a key.

Veils wear a shield of silence that only drops on beats 2 and 4 — strike in the gaps (Snare Arc loves them). Golden Notes sometimes flee across the field — catch one for a free harmony and a shower of Echoes.

Survive ten minutes to restore the song. Spend ◈ Echoes on permanent Resonance, unlock new Singers, and earn harder Songs with richer rewards. A new Daily Hum with a unique twist arrives every day.
